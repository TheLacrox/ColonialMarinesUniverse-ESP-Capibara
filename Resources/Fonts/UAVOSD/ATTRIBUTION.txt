UAV OSD Sans Mono
Designer: Nicholas Kruse
Source: https://www.dafont.com/uav-osd-sans.font
License note from source: 100% free; author note says free for personal or commercial use.

Modified for CMU-14 (es-ES support).
The upstream font maps only ASCII 32-126, so every Spanish character fell through
to the NotoSans fallback in StyleNano's uavOsdStack and rendered visibly smaller
and lowercase next to the all-caps OSD glyphs.

Added 18 glyphs, composed from the existing outlines rather than drawn from
scratch, so the OSD style is preserved:

  ÁÉÍÓÚ áéíóú   base letter + acute mark
  Ü ü           base letter + dieresis
  Ñ ñ           base letter + square tilde
  ¡ ¿           exclam / question rotated 180 degrees
  « »           two narrowed copies of less / greater

The letters are NOT compressed to make room: in an all-caps face a shortened
capital reads as a lowercase letter, which is the very defect being fixed. The
accents sit above the em box instead, and hhea.ascent / OS/2 usWinAscent /
OS/2 sTypoAscender were raised from 4096 to 5100 so they are not clipped.
